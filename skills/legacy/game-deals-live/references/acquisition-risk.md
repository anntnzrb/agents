# Acquisition Risk

Before comparing differing delivery mechanisms: price ≠ equivalence.

## Classification order

Use explicit current evidence. Multiple labels → mechanism buyer actually receives; unresolved → `unknown`.

`ownership_key`: Buyer receives key redeemed into buyer’s own platform account. Verify platform, DRM, activation country, edition, expiration, revocation/refund terms. “Key” ≠ authorization, provenance, or regional compatibility. Prefer official/authorized sellers when facts and totals otherwise comparable.

`direct_ownership`: Retailer grants entitlement directly to buyer’s own store/platform account. Verify purchase, not included subscription access. “Ownership” = durable license-like access, not legal property claim.

`gift`: Product arrives via gift or inventory transfer. Verify country compatibility, recipient eligibility, friendship/cooldown rules, delivery timing, reversal risk. Do not group gifts with ordinary keys without labeling transfer constraints.

`subscription_access`: Access depends on active subscription, catalog membership, tier, or online entitlement check. Verify tier, trial/intro price, billing interval, renewal price, catalog-removal risk, DLC coverage, save portability. NEVER describe as owning or keeping the game. May be absolute cheapest; cannot be cheapest ownership.

`account`: Seller provides, shares, transfers, or remotely activates an account instead of granting product to buyer’s existing account. Includes offline activation, preloaded accounts, credential delivery, shared accounts, seller-controlled family access. Flag recovery, lockout, ToS, security, privacy, multiplayer, cloud-save, resale/revocation risk. Treat acquisition terms as `blocked` when they prevent valid ownership comparison. NEVER recommend as equivalent to key or direct entitlement.

`bundle`: Offer contains multiple products/entitlements. Verify every current component, delivery mechanism, platform, regional restriction. Identify base game included, duplicated, excluded, or separately required. When known, expand underlying acquisition type in prose; `bundle` alone does not specify key vs direct entitlement.

`unknown`: Listing unreliably reveals delivery, entitlement, or product semantics. Keep in absolute-cheapest discovery without changing price order; label uncertainty; do not recommend until resolved.

## Offer type vs seller quality

Keep separate: acquisition mechanism; official/authorized status; product identity and component coverage; regional activation; evidence strength; checkout total.

Authorized subscription ≠ ownership. Cheap key may be region-locked. Verified product page may hide fees/tax.

## Edition and component risk

Marketing names ≠ stable contracts; verify current component list. “Deluxe” may contain cosmetics only; “Ultimate” may omit later DLC. Season pass may not include every expansion. Soundtrack or upgrade pack may match base title without containing game. Dynamic bundle may quote personalized, unreproducible price. Compare complete requested coverage, not product-name similarity.

## DRM and platform risk

Report exact redemption platform and required launcher. Distinguish DRM-free download; Steam/Epic/GOG/Microsoft entitlement; publisher launcher; external account linking. Verify cross-store DLC compatibility; DLC usually must match base game’s platform/store ecosystem. Without evidence, do not claim Steam Deck, OS, family-sharing, offline, multiplayer, or mod support.

## Pricing traps

Check: subscription trial/first-month pricing; membership-only price; coupon requiring account, app, newsletter, or payment method; bulk/mystery/random key; cash-back presented as immediate discount; tax/service fee omitted until checkout; foreign-currency conversion/issuer fee; out-of-stock/expired headline; base-game requirement hidden on DLC/upgrade page.

## Recommendation

Report absolute cheapest faithfully, then whether it satisfies intended acquisition. Recommend cheapest offer meeting product, ownership/access, region, platform, and evidence constraints. If none verified, say so and give safest next verification step.
