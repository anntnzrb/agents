# Progression reference

## Deterministic route
- Start Technique (Path of the Bastard) + Wintry Rapier; Chapter 5 Bone-Cutting Sawblade acquired → Sawblade + Booster Glaive route.
- Every Stargazer: exhaust NPC dialogue; revisit after major bosses.
- Reserve unique boss Ergo; keep records unspent until their checklist step; save before Sophia, Gepetto, and final-boss decisions.

## Branch protocol
Track every lie/truth response and compassion action; heart-pounding/white-hair Humanity signal; Julian and Cecile NPC rewards; Eugenie’s Alidoro answer; Sophia’s decision; Geppetto’s request; final boss.
- **Rise of P**: high Humanity; give Sophia peace; refuse Geppetto; defeat Nameless Puppet.
- **Free from the Puppet String**: insufficient Rise Humanity and/or let Sophia live; refuse Geppetto; defeat Nameless Puppet.
- **Real Boy**: give your heart to Geppetto; no Nameless Puppet fight.

Pre-choice cloud save: preserve before Sophia; complete each ending’s post-ending letters and piano interaction at Hotel Krat; reload for the next route. Start NG+ only after cleanup; NG+ supplies remaining gold/alternate records.

Overture has two verified outcomes, not generic “ending A/B” branches:
- Defeat Blood Artist → Rose’s Memory.
- Complete Lea’s route before the post-DLC Eventide Hotel Krat visit → hear the Legend’s last recording.
At Rose Garden Entrance, stop at the Point of No Return; before the finale, finish Tracker clues, records, gestures, NPC stories, and every other missable requirement.

After defeating Corrupted Parade Master in Chapter 9, use Star's Chrysalis at the Path of Pilgrim → enter Overture. Post-DLC, return through Eventide Hotel Krat for the Legend recording, ending weapon, and record.
