# Combat corpus

## Deterministic default
- Use Legendary Stalker; Technique lane; no Specters.
- Start with Path of the Bastard or Wintry Rapier.
- Acquisition order: Booster Glaive handle in Chapter 3 → Bone-Cutting Sawblade in Chapter 5's Malum District → assemble Sawblade + Booster Glaive only after both acquisitions.
- Keep Technique lane; use Booster handle for reach and charged-attack control; choose a Technique crank only when its scaling is confirmed.
- Optional progression, not requirements: Trident of the Covenant for a safer, high-reach moveset → Proof of Humanity after availability.
- Abrasives optional. When an abrasive is unavailable, use a physical weapon + guard/perfect-guard/stagger loop; NEVER require consumables, throwables, or Legion ammunition.

## Loop
1. Enter at full Pulse Cells; repair weapon durability.
2. Read one attack string; guard rather than repeatedly roll.
3. Perfect-guard multi-hit strings; use guard regain to recover lost health.
4. Charge heavy attacks after a whiff or staggerable recovery.
5. White stagger cue → charged heavy → fatal attack.
6. Heal after a confirmed recovery; NEVER during an untested string.

## Build priorities
- Vitality/Vigor first for survivability → Capacity for frame/liner/sleeve weight → Technique for the chosen handle.
- P-Organ priorities: guard regain; stagger window; Pulse Cell capacity/healing; perfect-guard utility.
- Aegis: defensive practice option. Falcon Eyes: optional ranged punctuation, never required.

## Taxonomy caveat
- Puppets generally susceptible to Electric Blitz; carcasses generally susceptible to fire. NEVER infer exact absorptions.
- Scrapped Watchman: sourced exception; absorbs acid weakness and resists Electric Blitz despite being a puppet.
- Use acid only when available; otherwise physical damage, guard, perfect guard, and punish timing are the non-consumable fallback.

## Boss plans
- All base and Overture encounters: same no-summon discipline—identify punishable recovery; preserve stamina; avoid greed during phase changes.
- Names, route order, and spoiler-sensitive counters belong in CLI spoiler-gated output.
- Confidence recorded per corpus entry; unknown exact absorptions remain unknown.
- Sources: `official_patch_1_12`, `fextra_bosses`, `fextra_weapons`, `powerpyx_base`, `powerpyx_overture` in `resources/source_registry.json`.

## Source-layer policy
- Community corpus: sentiment and dissent, not mechanics.
- Official patch notes and cross-checked wiki facts: identity/availability layers.
- Reddit, Steam, and calculator repositories: anecdotal research.
- NEVER convert a preference into an exact AR, scaling, absorption, or DPS claim.
- Recommendations cautious; spoiler gates preserved, especially for Nameless Puppet, Laxasia, King of Puppets/Romeo, and Overture bosses Arlecchino and Tyrannical Predator.
- Displayed comparator: may add physical and elemental displayed AR, then apply explicitly supplied retention and motion assumptions. Excludes enemy defense, hidden scaling or saturation, animation DPS, status buildup, and Fable effects; no licensed validated exact formula was found.
- Community claims, such as Trident or Proof of Humanity's sourced 30% critical chance, remain attributed context, not a universal performance guarantee.
