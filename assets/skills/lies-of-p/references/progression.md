# Progression reference

## Deterministic route
Start with Technique (Path of the Bastard) and the Wintry Rapier; after acquiring the Bone-Cutting Sawblade in Chapter 5, assemble the Sawblade + Booster Glaive route. At every Stargazer, exhaust NPC dialogue and revisit after major bosses. Reserve unique boss Ergo, keep records unspent until their checklist step, and save before Sophia, Gepetto, and final-boss decisions.

## Branch protocol
Track every lie/truth response and compassion action, including the heart-pounding/white-hair Humanity signal, plus the Julian and Cecile NPC rewards, Eugenie’s Alidoro answer, Sophia’s decision, Geppetto’s request, and the final boss. For **Rise of P**, require high Humanity, give Sophia peace, refuse Geppetto, and defeat Nameless Puppet. For **Free from the Puppet String**, have insufficient Rise Humanity and/or let Sophia live, then refuse Geppetto and defeat Nameless Puppet. For **Real Boy**, give your heart to Geppetto (no Nameless Puppet fight).

A pre-choice cloud save supports alternate endings: preserve it before Sophia, complete each ending’s post-ending letters and piano interaction at Hotel Krat, then reload for the next route. Start NG+ only after that cleanup; NG+ supplies remaining gold/alternate records.

Overture has two verified outcomes rather than generic “ending A/B” branches: defeat Blood Artist for Rose’s Memory, and complete Lea’s route before the post-DLC Eventide Hotel Krat visit to hear the Legend’s last recording. At Rose Garden Entrance, stop at the Point of No Return and finish Tracker clues, records, gestures, NPC stories, and every other missable requirement before the finale.

After defeating Corrupted Parade Master in Chapter 9, use Star's Chrysalis at the Path of Pilgrim to enter Overture. Post-DLC, return through Eventide Hotel Krat for the Legend recording, ending weapon, and record.
