# References

The corpus is normalized from the source ledger in `resources/source_registry.json`. High-impact version and trophy facts use official patch notes and PowerPyx guides; boss and weapon cross-checks use Fextra. Community/video links are retained as corroboration only and no prose or unlicensed datasets are copied.

- `official_patch_1_12`: official 1.12 notes; primary version delta
- `psnprofiles_platinum_pdf`: independent platinum checklist cross-check
- `powerpyx_base`, `powerpyx_overture`: base and Overture trophy/route references
- `fextra_bosses`, `fextra_weapons`: boss, area, and weapon names
- `youtube_1`–`youtube_6`: gameplay corroboration links; confidence is not elevated solely by video
- `reddit_403_attempts`: Reddit access attempts recorded; 403 means no claims were copied
- `github_repos`: repository search retained for provenance; no code/data copied
- `context7_irrelevant`: irrelevant lookup retained for audit transparency

Exact elemental absorption values are intentionally marked unknown where the cited sources do not establish them.
