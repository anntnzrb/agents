# Acquisition Risk

Read this before comparing offers with different delivery mechanisms. Price alone is not equivalence.

## Classification order

Classify from explicit current evidence. If multiple labels appear, choose the mechanism the buyer actually receives; use `unknown` when unresolved.

### `ownership_key`

- Buyer receives a key redeemed into the buyer’s own platform account
- Verify platform, DRM, activation country, edition, expiration, and revocation/refund terms
- “Key” does not prove authorization, provenance, or regional compatibility
- Prefer official/authorized sellers when facts and totals are otherwise comparable

### `direct_ownership`

- Retailer grants the entitlement directly to the buyer’s own store/platform account
- Verify the product is a purchase, not included subscription access
- Treat “ownership” as shorthand for durable license-like access, not a legal property claim

### `gift`

- Product arrives through gift or inventory transfer
- Verify country compatibility, recipient eligibility, friendship/cooldown rules, delivery timing, and reversal risk
- Do not group gifts with ordinary keys without labeling the transfer constraints

### `subscription_access`

- Access depends on an active subscription, catalog membership, tier, or online entitlement check
- Verify tier, trial/intro price, billing interval, renewal price, catalog-removal risk, DLC coverage, and save portability
- Never describe this as owning or keeping the game
- It may be absolute cheapest but cannot be cheapest ownership

### `account`

- Seller provides, shares, transfers, or remotely activates an account rather than granting the product to the buyer’s existing account
- Include offline activation, preloaded accounts, credential delivery, shared accounts, and seller-controlled family access
- Flag recovery, lockout, ToS, security, privacy, multiplayer, cloud-save, and resale/revocation risk
- Treat acquisition terms as `blocked` when they prevent a valid ownership comparison
- Do not recommend an account offer as equivalent to a key or direct entitlement

### `bundle`

- Offer contains multiple products or entitlements
- Verify every current component, delivery mechanism, platform, and regional restriction
- Identify whether the base game is included, duplicated, excluded, or required separately
- Expand the underlying acquisition type in prose when known; `bundle` alone does not describe key versus direct entitlement

### `unknown`

- The listing does not reveal delivery, entitlement, or product semantics reliably
- Keep it in absolute-cheapest discovery without changing price order, label the uncertainty, and do not recommend it until resolved

## Offer type versus seller quality

Keep these axes separate:

- acquisition mechanism
- official/authorized status
- product identity and component coverage
- regional activation
- evidence strength
- checkout total

An authorized subscription is still not ownership. A cheap key may still be region-locked. A verified product page may still hide fees or tax.

## Edition and component risk

- Marketing names are not stable contracts. Verify the current component list
- “Deluxe” may contain cosmetics only; “Ultimate” may omit later DLC
- A season pass may not include every expansion
- A soundtrack or upgrade pack can match the base title while not containing the game
- A dynamic bundle can quote a personalized price that another buyer cannot reproduce
- Compare complete requested coverage, not product-name similarity

## DRM and platform risk

- Report the exact redemption platform and required launcher
- Distinguish DRM-free download, Steam/Epic/GOG/Microsoft entitlement, publisher launcher, and external account linking
- Verify cross-store DLC compatibility; DLC usually must match the base game’s platform/store ecosystem
- Do not claim Steam Deck, OS, family-sharing, offline, multiplayer, or mod support without evidence

## Pricing traps

Check for:

- subscription trial or first-month pricing
- membership-only price
- coupon requiring account, app, newsletter, or payment method
- bulk/mystery/random key
- cash-back presented as immediate discount
- tax or service fee omitted until checkout
- foreign-currency conversion and issuer fee
- out-of-stock or expired headline
- base-game requirement hidden on DLC/upgrade pages

## Recommendation rule

Report absolute cheapest faithfully, then state whether it satisfies the user’s intended acquisition. Recommend the cheapest offer that meets product, ownership/access, region, platform, and evidence constraints. If none is verified, say so and give the safest next verification step.
